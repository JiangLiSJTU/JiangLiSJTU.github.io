证据边界Evidence boundary

PyTorch官方回顾及大会报道展示的是HyperParallel整体成果，不宜表述为“PyTorch单独发布HyperOffload”。更准确的说法是：PyTorch Conference China 2026公开展示了HyperParallel；大会架构图包含Offload模块；MindSpore官方资料和技术报告进一步确认HyperOffload是HyperParallel核心技术之一，并记录了上海交通大学蒋力、刘方鑫团队与华为的联合研发成果。

The PyTorch recap and conference reports present HyperParallel as a whole; they should not be described as a standalone PyTorch release of HyperOffload. A precise statement is that HyperParallel was publicly presented at PyTorch Conference China 2026, its stage architecture included an Offload module, and official MindSpore materials and the technical report identify HyperOffload as a core HyperParallel technology jointly developed by the Shanghai Jiao Tong University team and Huawei.
